# 📊 IBM Telco Customer Churn Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes customer churn for a telecommunications company using the IBM Telco Customer Churn dataset. The objective is to understand customer churn behavior, identify high-risk customer segments, analyze the factors contributing to customer attrition, and evaluate the potential revenue impact of churn.

The project includes data preparation, data modeling, DAX-based analysis, and an interactive Power BI dashboard.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze customer churn patterns
- Identify high-risk customer segments
- Understand key factors influencing customer churn
- Analyze churn across customer tenure cohorts
- Evaluate Customer Lifetime Value (CLTV)
- Analyze Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR)
- Identify Monthly Recurring Revenue at Risk (MRR at Risk)
- Develop customer risk analysis
- Generate actionable business insights for customer retention

## 🔄 Data Preparation & Modeling

Multiple customer datasets were integrated to create a structured analytical model.

The project used a Snowflake Schema to establish relationships between customer, demographic, location, population, service, and churn-related data.

Key preparation steps included:

- Data cleaning and validation
- Handling missing values
- Removing unnecessary fields
- Establishing table relationships
- Creating calculated DAX measures
- Preparing data for visualization and analysis

## 🔍 Key Insights

### Customer Churn
The analysis identified overall churn patterns and highlighted customer segments contributing significantly to customer attrition.

### Contract Type
Month-to-month customers showed a higher tendency to churn compared with customers on longer-term contracts.

### Customer Tenure
Customers with lower tenure were more vulnerable to churn, highlighting the importance of effective onboarding and early customer engagement.

### Services
Churn behavior varied across different internet and service categories, helping identify customer groups that may require targeted retention strategies.

### Payment Methods
Churn patterns differed across payment methods, providing another useful dimension for customer segmentation.

### Customer Lifetime Value
CLTV analysis helped identify higher-value customers and provides a basis for prioritizing retention efforts.

### MRR & MRR at Risk
Monthly Recurring Revenue and MRR at Risk were analyzed to understand the potential financial impact of customer churn.

### Customer Risk Score
A DAX-based Risk Score was developed to identify customers who may require proactive retention efforts.

### Churn Reasons
Churn reasons were analyzed to understand the major factors behind customer attrition and identify areas for improvement.

### Tenure Cohorts
Customers were grouped into tenure cohorts to understand how churn behavior changes throughout the customer lifecycle.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis:

- Prioritize high-risk customers for retention campaigns
- Focus retention efforts on high-value customers
- Improve onboarding for newly acquired customers
- Develop targeted strategies for month-to-month customers
- Address major customer pain points identified through churn reasons
- Monitor MRR at Risk to identify potential revenue loss
- Use customer risk scores for proactive retention
- Develop personalized retention strategies based on customer segments
